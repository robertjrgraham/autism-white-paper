What Causes Autism?

A Comprehensive Root Cause Analysis Based on 301 Research Papers

Version 2.0 • May 31, 2026 • Multi-Toxicant Cascade Model • Susceptible Subgroup Hypothesis

Abstract: Autism is the result of multi-toxicant environmental exposure during critical neurodevelopmental windows, overwhelming a genetically susceptible child's detoxification capacity, and triggering a self-amplifying cascade encompassing gut dysbiosis, immune dysregulation, cholinergic dysfunction, neuroinflammation, mitochondrial failure, excitotoxicity, and epigenetic reprogramming.

Key Conclusions

  • Three factors required: toxicant exposure + genetic susceptibility + critical developmental timing
  • MTHFR, GST, MT, CHRNA7 variants create a susceptible subgroup (~10-20% of population)
  • Cholinergic dysfunction (nAChR impairment) is a newly integrated mechanism — both genetic and acquired via heavy metal inhibition
  • Epigenetic changes are potentially reversible with intensive treatment
  • 98 citations linking every claim to its source paper
