## What We Found

The evidence points to a three-factor model:

1. **Toxicant exposure** — Ethylmercury from vaccine thimerosal, aluminum adjuvants, maternal amalgams
2. **Genetic susceptibility** — MTHFR, GST, MT, and CHRNA7 variants create a susceptible subgroup (~10-20% of population)
3. **Critical timing** — First 18 months of neurodevelopment, when detox systems are immature

Together, these trigger a 6-step cascade:

- **Step 1:** Thimerosal + aluminum overwhelm immature detox systems
- **Step 2:** Heavy metals inhibit nAChRs (especially α7) — cholinergic dysfunction impairs neurodevelopment and sensory gating
- **Step 3:** Mercury disrupts gut flora, enables pathogens, compromises gut barrier
- **Step 4:** Chronic microglial activation, neuroinflammation, immune dysregulation
- **Step 5:** Mitochondrial electron transport chain inhibition → energy deficit
- **Step 6:** GABA/glutamate imbalance, excitotoxicity, disrupted synaptic pruning

## Why This Matters

When a family hears "Level 3 autism," they're handed a diagnosis and a waitlist. They're not handed a coherent model of what's happening biologically or a staged plan for what to do about it. This package is designed to fill that gap — not as medical advice, but as a starting point synthesized from 24 years of published research.

The cascade model means treatments can be targeted at multiple intervention points simultaneously. The protocol phases treatments realistically: foundation first, active treatment second, advanced options third.
